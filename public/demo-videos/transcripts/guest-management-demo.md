# Guest Management

Audience: Planners who currently live in guest spreadsheets.
Target duration: 60 seconds

## Script

### 0:00-0:14

Action: Open Guest Management and inspect invitation, response, attendance, and plus-one totals.

This is the planner’s guest operating table: invitations, responses, attendance, and plus-ones — the entire list with its current state visible in one view.

### 0:14-0:36

Action: Filter to Declined and inspect the RSVP, group, and plus-one columns preserved in the table.

Filtering to Declined narrows the work without losing the picture: every declined guest keeps RSVP, group, and plus-one context in the table, so follow-up happens with full information instead of a stripped-down list.

### 0:36-1:00

Action: Clear the RSVP filter, switch to Card View, and inspect the collapsed Priya Shah card.

Card View turns the same data into a briefing. Priya Shah’s card reads at a glance — confirmed, Friends group, vegetarian meal, one submitted plus-one — everything a planner needs to know about a guest, in one readable place.
