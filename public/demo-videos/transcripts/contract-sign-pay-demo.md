# Vendor Contracts -> Payment Milestones

Audience: Planners who need vendor agreements, payment milestones, and wedding budget context in one workflow.
Target duration: 50 seconds

## Script

### 0:00-0:12

Action: Open the existing planner contract and inspect its details and status.

A vendor agreement starts from the record the planner already owns: the vendor, the value, the status, and the key dates, together in one place.

### 0:12-0:30

Action: Open Contract Sections, then Payments, and inspect the seeded clauses and milestones.

Contract Sections renders the scope, clauses, and terms as a readable canvas, and Payments keeps the deposit and remaining milestones attached to the same agreement — not a document in one tool and a spreadsheet in another.

### 0:30-0:50

Action: Return to the wedding budget and inspect the vendor commitment in its category context.

The wedding budget carries that same commitment into the complete financial picture — planned versus actual by category, with the vendor’s numbers where the planner expects them. Agreements and budget stay one connected story.
