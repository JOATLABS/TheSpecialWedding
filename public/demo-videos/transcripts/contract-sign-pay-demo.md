# Vendor Contracts -> Payment Milestones

Audience: Planners who need vendor agreements, payment milestones, and wedding budget context in one workflow.
Target duration: 50 seconds

## Script

### 0:00-0:12

Action: Open the existing planner contract and inspect its details and status.

A vendor agreement starts from the record the planner already owns.

### 0:12-0:30

Action: Open Contract Sections, then Payments, and inspect the seeded clauses and milestones.

Scope, clauses, deliverables, and payment terms stay connected instead of splitting into another document tool.

### 0:30-0:50

Action: Return to the wedding budget and inspect the vendor commitment in its category context.

The wedding budget carries that same vendor commitment into the planner's complete financial picture.
