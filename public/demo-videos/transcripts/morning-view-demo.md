# Morning View

Audience: Planners who want proof the dashboard replaces a morning spreadsheet sweep.
Target duration: 60 seconds

## Script

### 0:00-0:12

Action: Open the planner dashboard and pause on the active wedding summary.

This is the 8am view: every wedding that needs attention, without opening a spreadsheet.

### 0:12-0:36

Action: Compare the visible RSVP, activity, and pending-form signals on the two wedding cards.

RSVP movement, recent activity, pending forms, and setup progress distinguish what is moving from what needs attention.

### 0:36-1:00

Action: Open Avery & Morgan's source record and inspect the remaining setup guidance.

The workflow does not end at insight. It points the planner to the next action that moves a real wedding forward.
