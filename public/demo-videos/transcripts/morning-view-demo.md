# Morning View

Audience: Planners who want proof the dashboard replaces a morning spreadsheet sweep.
Target duration: 60 seconds

## Script

### 0:00-0:12

Action: Open the planner dashboard and pause on the active wedding summary.

This is the eight a-m view: every active wedding, its current status, and what needs attention first — before a single spreadsheet gets opened.

### 0:12-0:36

Action: Compare the visible RSVP, activity, and pending-form signals on the two wedding cards.

Two weddings, two different mornings. Sofia and Daniel are moving: RSVPs arriving, recent activity, setup nearly complete. Avery and Morgan need attention — pending forms and unfinished setup, surfaced right on the card. The dashboard separates what is moving from what needs a planner today.

### 0:36-1:00

Action: Open Avery & Morgan's source record and inspect the remaining setup guidance.

Insight is not the end of the workflow. Opening Avery and Morgan’s record turns attention into the next concrete action: the setup guide names the remaining work, so the morning starts with a decision instead of a hunt — and a real wedding moves forward.
