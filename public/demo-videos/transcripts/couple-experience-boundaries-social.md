# A clear couple experience. Planner-owned boundaries.

Audience: Wedding planners who want clients informed without exposing internal operations.
Target duration: 20 seconds

## Script

### 0:00-0:05

Action: Hold on the planner-controlled couple intake form.

Clear boundaries start with a workflow the planner owns.

### 0:05-0:10

Action: Switch manually to the assigned form in the couple portal.

The couple gets a guided next step, not the planner’s internal controls.

### 0:10-0:15

Action: Open the couple Payments view and hold on the shared schedule.

The answers clients need stay available without another midnight text.
