7. Customer-Facing Risk Assessment

High-Risk Issues (User-Visible Impact)
Risk: 14-Day Inactivity Exit Behavior Unclear

Issue: Users marked inactive (14+ days) are set to reengagement_pending status, but the sequence does not actually terminate. They could continue receiving emails.

Customer Impact: Inactive users who explicitly don't want emails could be frustrated by continued sends. Could increase unsubscribe rates without warning.

Likelihood: Medium (depends on how many users become inactive)

Risk: Email Send Failures Not Visibly Handled

Issue: If an email fails to send (network issue, invalid email, service down), the claim is released and sequence can retry, but there's no visible notification to the planner.

Customer Impact: Planners may not realize they missed a critical onboarding email. Could lead to support tickets about missing Day 1 email.

Likelihood: Low-Medium (email service availability is typically high)

Medium-Risk Issues (Potential Confusion)
Risk: Comparison Page Confidence Levels May Be Misunderstood

Issue: Confidence levels (High/Medium/Low) in the evidence table may not be immediately clear to non-technical planners. "High confidence" can still mean a feature is "not found."

Customer Impact: Planner may misread "High confidence" in the "Not found" row as a positive feature claim.

Likelihood: Medium (depends on user attention to detail)

Risk: Unsubscribe Token Expiration Not User-Facing

Issue: After 90 days, an unsubscribe link stops working without explanation. A user might attempt to unsubscribe and get a 404.

Customer Impact: Planner who wishes to unsubscribe after 3+ months receives no feedback. Could feel ignored.

Likelihood: Low (most unsubscribe within first few weeks)

Low-Risk Issues (Minor UX Friction)
Risk: No Rate Limit Error Messaging

Issue: Rate limit hit on unsubscribe endpoints returns 429 with retry_after, but error message is generic ("Too many requests").

Customer Impact: If a planner submits unsubscribe form multiple times quickly, they'll see a rate limit error with no explanation.

Likelihood: Very Low (requires repeated rapid clicks)

Risk: Email Display Name Escaping May Look Odd

Issue: HTML entities in user display names (e.g., "John & Jane") may render as "&amp;" in plaintext email clients.

Customer Impact: In plaintext email view, display name might show escaped HTML.

Likelihood: Low (most email clients support HTML)

8. Recommendations for Improving Customer Experience

Priority: High
Action: Clarify the 14-day inactivity exit behavior.
Details: Decide whether reengagement_pending status should prevent future sends or allow a "come back" email after inactivity. Currently, the behavior is ambiguous and could lead to frustrated users. Document the decision in code comments.
Priority: High
Action: Add comprehensive test coverage for Days 3, 5, 8, 12 emails.
Details: Write dedicated tests for later sequence steps to ensure timing, skip conditions, and HTML rendering are correct across the full 12-day journey. Use the Day 1 pattern as a template.
Priority: Medium
Action: Improve email send failure visibility.
Details: Add optional logging or alerting when email sends fail after 2-3 retries. Consider a dashboard view showing which planners missed emails in their onboarding sequence.
Priority: Medium
Action: Clarify comparison page confidence terminology.
Details: Update the Evidence table to use more explicit language. Instead of "Confidence: High / Not found," use "Source Status: Confirmed / Not found in reviewed sources." Add a legend explaining confidence levels.
Priority: Medium
Action: Extend unsubscribe token TTL or allow re-request after expiration.
Details: Either increase from 90 days to 180+ days, or show a friendly "token expired; request a new one" page that allows the user to enter their email and get a fresh unsubscribe link.
Priority: Low
Action: Improve rate limit error messaging on public endpoints.
Details: Update 429 response to include a user-friendly message: "You've submitted this form too quickly. Please wait a few seconds and try again." Include the retry_after value in a more human-readable format.
Priority: Low
Action: Test comparison page with screen readers and accessibility tools.
Details: Run WCAG audit on /compare/[competitor] pages to ensure evidence tables, source links, and confidence badges are accessible to planners using assistive technology.
