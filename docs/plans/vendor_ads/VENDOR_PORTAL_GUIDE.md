# The Special Wedding — Vendor Portal User Guide

Welcome to The Special Wedding vendor portal! This guide will help you set up your account, create campaigns, and manage your advertising presence on our wedding planning platform.

## Getting Started

### Creating Your Account

To get started, contact **ads@thespecialwedding.io** to request a vendor account, or our admin team will create one for you. Once your account is ready, you'll receive credentials via email.

### Logging In

Visit **https://thespecialwedding.io/portal/ads** and log in with your email and password. This login is separate from planner accounts and is reserved exclusively for vendors.

### Your Dashboard

Upon logging in, you'll see your overview dashboard with key performance indicators:
- **Campaigns**: Number of active campaigns
- **Impressions**: Total times your ads have been viewed
- **Clicks**: Total clicks on your ads
- **Leads**: Inquiries from engaged planners
- **CTR (Click-Through Rate)**: Percentage of impressions that resulted in clicks

## Creating Your First Campaign

Navigate to **Campaigns** and click **Create Campaign** to launch our 6-step wizard. Each step builds on the previous one.

### Step 1: Goal Selection

Choose what you want to achieve:
- **Profile Visits**: Drive planners to learn more about your business
- **Quote Requests**: Receive direct inquiries from planners
- **Bookings**: Encourage planners to book your services

### Step 2: Targeting

Narrow your audience to reach the right couples:
- **Categories**: Select which vendor categories apply (venue, photographer, catering, florist, etc.)
- **Regions**: Choose geographic areas where you serve
- **Placements**: Control where your ads appear (dashboard, vendors page, budget planner)

### Step 3: Budget

Set your spending limits:
- **Daily Budget**: Maximum you'll spend per day
- **Total Budget**: Cap for the entire campaign
- **Start Date**: When your campaign goes live
- **End Date**: When your campaign stops

### Step 4: Pricing

Currently, all campaigns run on our **free tier** at no cost. We track all engagement metrics (impressions, clicks, leads) for you to measure performance. Premium billing options with Stripe integration are coming soon.

### Step 5: Creative

Upload and write the content that planners will see:
- **Headline**: Up to 100 characters; make it attention-grabbing and clear
- **Description**: Up to 300 characters; highlight your unique value
- **Offer**: Up to 200 characters; include any special promotions or incentives
- **Call-to-Action (CTA)**: Up to 50 characters; tell them what to do next (e.g., "Get Quote")
- **Image**: Upload a high-quality photo or graphic that represents your business

### Step 6: Review & Submit

Review all your choices. Once you're satisfied, click **Submit**. Your campaign moves to pending review and will be live within 24–48 hours (after admin approval).

## Campaign Lifecycle

Understanding campaign statuses helps you manage your advertising:

- **Draft**: Your campaign is saved but not yet submitted. You can edit all settings and creatives freely.
- **Pending Review**: You've submitted your campaign and our team is reviewing it for quality and compliance.
- **Active**: Your campaign is live and visible to planners across the platform.
- **Paused**: Your campaign is temporarily stopped (by you or an admin). You can resume it anytime.
- **Completed**: Your campaign's end date has passed and it is no longer active.
- **Rejected**: Our team rejected your campaign (we'll include detailed notes). Make edits and resubmit for approval.

## Managing Creatives

### Creative Library

Visit **Creatives** to view, edit, and manage all the images and content you've created across campaigns.

### Image Requirements

Upload clear, professional images in JPG or PNG format. Images should be at least 400×400 pixels and no larger than 5 MB. Avoid blurry, cluttered, or misleading photos.

### Setting Your Primary Creative

Your primary creative is shown first when planners view your campaign. You can change this anytime by selecting a different creative and marking it as primary.

### Creative Review Process

When you create or update a creative, it enters a review queue. Status progresses from **Pending** → **Approved** or **Rejected**. Rejected creatives include feedback; make changes and resubmit.

## Viewing Leads

### Leads Page

Visit **Leads** to see all inquiries from engaged planners. Leads come in two types:

- **Quote Request**: A planner sent you a direct message asking for a quote or more information.
- **Add to Directory**: A planner saved your profile for future reference.

### Responding to Quotes

When you receive a quote request, reply directly from your own email. We've configured Reply-To so your response goes straight to the planner. Respond promptly—couples often compare multiple vendors.

### Marking Leads as Responded

Once you've contacted a planner, mark the lead as **Responded** in the portal to keep your inbox organized and track your follow-up efforts.

## Billing

### Current Pricing

You're currently on the **free tier** at no cost. We track all impressions, clicks, and leads so you can measure ROI without any upfront investment.

### Future Billing Options

We're rolling out **CPC (Cost-Per-Click)** and **CPL (Cost-Per-Lead)** billing models with Stripe integration. You'll receive advance notice before any changes.

### Billing Page

Visit **Billing** to view your current tracked metrics, spending summary, and payment history. This page is your resource for understanding your account's financial status.

## Tips for Success

**Write Compelling Headlines**: Grab attention in 100 characters or less. Use numbers, questions, or benefit-driven statements.

**Use High-Quality Images**: A professional photo builds trust and stands out in planners' feeds.

**Target Relevant Categories**: The more precise your targeting, the more engaged your audience will be.

**Monitor Your Lead Response Time**: Couples book vendors who respond quickly. Aim to reply within 24 hours.
