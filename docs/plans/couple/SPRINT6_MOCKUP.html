❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What should I wear?

We recommend semi-formal attire. Think cocktail dress or nice slacks with a button-down shirt. Our color palette is dusty rose and sage green if you'd like to coordinate!

Where is the venue?

The Grand Ballroom, 123 Wedding Ave, Chicago, IL 60601. Complimentary valet parking is included.

Get Directions ↗

Can I bring a plus one?

Plus ones are by invitation only. Check your RSVP to see if yours is included. If you have questions, contact your planner.

Are there dietary accommodations?

Yes! Please note any dietary restrictions in your RSVP form. We'll have vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free options available.
