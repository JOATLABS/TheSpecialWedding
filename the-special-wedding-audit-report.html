2. UX Copy Review

Summary

Strengths: The hero copy is clear and compelling. "The all-in-one platform for professional wedding planners" immediately communicates the value prop. The replacement pills (crossing out competitor names) is a smart pattern. Section bridges ("See what's inside", "Find your fit") create a natural reading rhythm.

Top improvements: Several CTAs are generic ("Start your free trial" appears 4 times), the pricing tier features lack hierarchy cues, and the hero subhead buries the new headline features (Couple Portal, SMS, AI) in a list.

Detailed Findings
